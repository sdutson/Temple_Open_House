How to share a pass-along card

Your bishop and ward leaders have the cards. The cards are a tool for a personal invitation. They are not meant to be picked up from a table and taken home.

  1. Pray about who to invite

    Think of someone you already know: a neighbor, a coworker, a friend from the team or the PTA.

  2. Hand them a card and invite them in person

    For most of our neighbors this is the only time they can ever walk through a temple. Offer to go with them.

    Try: "I'd love for you to come see this with me."

  3. Keep inviting through November

    Mention it again, share your own experience, and post to your ward or family group using the files below.

The youth and Primary challenge

Half of the stake's cards are set aside for Young Women, Young Men, and Primary children. The stake presidency has approved a friendly challenge for them to out-invite the adults. Our bet is on the kids.
